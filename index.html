A Quiet Letter

Open when you're alone. No need to reply.

I probably won't send this text, just like the many other texts I've typed and ended up deleting before sending.

I guess I just need somewhere to put all the things I wish I could tell you, without actually having to tell you.

I'm sorry for how things turned out between us. I'm sorry for the things I said in the heat of the moment, especially the ones that hurt you. I never wanted to be the reason you felt hurt, and I hate knowing that I might have caused you pain.

I think one thing about me is that when someone I love is upset with me, I struggle to be okay until I know they're okay too. When you withdraw, I feel this overwhelming need to explain myself, to apologise, to make sure you understand that I didn't mean to hurt you. It's like every step you take backwards makes me want to take four steps towards you. Not because I want to suffocate you or make you feel like you can't have your space, but because somewhere in my mind, I feel like if I don't fix things immediately, I might lose you.

And I know that's something I need to work on.

I don't want to become someone who makes you feel emotionally exhausted. I don't want my need to explain myself to become a burden to you. Sometimes I worry that I'll keep trying so hard to make things right that, one day, you'll get tired of me. That you'll slowly stop loving me, not because I don't love you enough, but because I don't know how to handle the fear of losing someone I love.

And honestly, that scares me.

I don't want you to think that every time I apologise, I'm trying to force you to forgive me or forget what happened. Sometimes I genuinely feel sorry. Sometimes I just wish you weren't hurting. And sometimes, if I'm being completely honest with myself, I don't know how to sit with the thought of you being upset with me. I want to make it better immediately, even when I know I should step back.

I'm learning that I can't keep doing that. I can't keep trying to manage your feelings just because I'm uncomfortable knowing that you're hurt. You're allowed to be upset. You're allowed to need space. And I'm allowed to feel sad about it without turning that sadness into another message.

I think I just wish I knew how to love you without being so afraid of losing you...

Because despite everything, I never wanted us to become strangers over an argument. I never wanted our last conversation to be filled with hurt, misunderstandings and words we couldn't take back. I wanted us to understand each other, even when we disagreed. I wanted us to be okay.

But I also understand that wanting something doesn't mean I can make it happen.

I know I can't force you to talk to me. I can't make you accept my apology before you're ready, and I can't keep explaining myself until all the hurt disappears. I've said what I could say, and now I need to learn to leave things where they are.

I still care about you. I still wish you peace. I still hope that, even in this silence, you don't think I ever wanted anything bad for you.

And maybe a part of me still wishes that one day we could have a calm conversation, understand what happened and part ways peacefully, or find our way forward if that's what we both genuinely want. I don't know what the future holds, and I'm trying not to write a whole story in my head about it.

For now, I need to respect the space we've agreed to take. Not because I suddenly stopped caring, but because caring about someone also means respecting what they've asked for.

I need to learn that I can be sorry without apologising a hundred times. That I can miss someone without reaching out. That someone can be upset with me without it meaning I'm unlovable. And that I don't have to fix everything tonight.

Maybe I'll never send this. Maybe tomorrow I'll read it and realise there were even more things I wanted to say. Maybe I'll write another one and delete it, too.

But at least here, I can be honest.

I loved you. I care about you. I'm sorry for the ways I hurt you 🥹

And I'm learning that I can wish you well, even when I don't know what happens next...

I hope you're okay. And I hope I learn to be okay, too.

I'm learning that love sometimes means being quiet so the other person can breathe. Even when your own heart is screaming. Even when every instinct tells you to reach out and ask: are we still okay?
